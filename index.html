About us

A cleaning company that treats your property like its own.

Hussain Projects Ltd is a professional cleaning company serving landlords, letting agents, property managers, businesses and schools. We handle end of tenancy deep cleans, HMO cleans, Airbnb turnarounds, offices and school premises — working directly with clients or as a trusted subcontractor.

Our approach is simple: turn up on time, follow your brief, pay attention to the detail, and tell you clearly what's been done. Every job is cleaned room by room to a consistent standard, so you know exactly what to expect.

  • Reliable attendance and punctuality
  • Professional, respectful conduct on site
  • Consistent standards, room by room
  • Following site-specific instructions
  • Responsible handling of keys and access
  • Confidentiality and respect for your property
